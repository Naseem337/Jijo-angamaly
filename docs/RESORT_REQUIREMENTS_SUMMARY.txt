RESORT WHATSAPP BOOKING ASSISTANT: REQUIREMENTS SUMMARY
Updated: 2026-10-28 (draft, to be confirmed with the client)

WHAT WE ARE BUILDING
A WhatsApp assistant for resorts and staycations, plus a simple management dashboard.
Guests chat on WhatsApp, pick their stay, pay online, and get a confirmed booking, with no phone calls
or manual follow-up needed. The resort team manages everything from the dashboard.

FIRST PROPERTIES
- Pozhiyoram Resort
- Vibes Kanthalloor
One owner manages several properties. More can be added later.

WHAT THE GUEST EXPERIENCE LOOKS LIKE
1. The guest messages the resort's WhatsApp number, by text or voice note.
2. The assistant replies in the guest's own language: English, Malayalam, Tamil or Hindi.
   It also understands mixed styles such as Manglish, Tanglish and Hinglish. Voice notes work
   both ways: the guest can send a voice note and get a voice reply.
3. The assistant offers ready-made options to tap, so guests don't have to type everything:
   - Which property (for example Pozhiyoram Resort or Vibes Kanthalloor)
   - Check-in and check-out dates
   - Number of guests (adults and children), with an option to choose a higher number
   - Number of rooms
   - Room type, with the total price for the stay
4. Room options are checked against capacity. If a guest tries to book a large group into one room,
   that option is not offered. The assistant shows the number of rooms the group actually needs.
5. The assistant checks live room availability and rates from the dashboard, and never guesses.
6. The guest confirms, and the rooms are held for a short time (about 15 minutes).
7. The guest receives a Razorpay payment link and a UPI QR code, and can pay the advance or the full amount.
8. After payment, the guest automatically receives a WhatsApp message:
   - Payment received and booking confirmed
   - Payment failed, with a link to try again
   - Payment pending or room on hold, please wait
   If the payment is not completed in time, the rooms are released.
9. The assistant can send room and property photos.
10. For anything it cannot handle (special requests, complaints, refunds), it hands the chat to the resort
    staff with a short summary of the conversation.

WHAT THE RESORT TEAM GETS (DASHBOARD)
- A clean, simple, easy-to-use screen, in the style of the Frappe desk (sidebar, lists, forms, calendar).
- Manage properties, room types, rooms, rates and seasonal prices.
- Booking calendar showing availability, holds, paid and cancelled bookings.
- Guest records with full stay history. Previous guests are stored automatically.
- View WhatsApp conversations and staff handoff requests.
- Login for the owner and managers.

OFFERS AND REPEAT GUESTS
- Past guests who agreed to receive messages can get offers, seasonal packages or availability
  alerts on WhatsApp.
- Guests can stop messages at any time by replying STOP.

HOW IT IS SET UP
- Testing first on a WhatsApp test number, using free-tier tools, so nothing is spent or risked.
- Then we connect the resort's own WhatsApp Business number and approve the message templates.
- Razorpay is connected with test keys first, then the resort's live account.

WHAT WE NEED FROM THE CLIENT
- Room types for each property, how many people each room fits, and photos
- Rates, including weekday, weekend and season prices, and whether GST is included
- Advance payment amount (or full payment) and cancellation and refund policy
- Check-in and check-out times
- Staff WhatsApp numbers for each property
- Razorpay account details
- WhatsApp Business account access for the final number
