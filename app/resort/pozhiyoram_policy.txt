Cancellation Policy

🌿 Cancellation made 7 or more days prior to arrival - 10% retention charge on total booking amount.

🌿 Cancellation made between 7 and 3 days prior to arrival - 25% retention charge.

🌿 Cancellation made between 3 and 2 days prior to arrival - 50% retention charge.

🌿 Cancellation made less than 48 hours prior to arrival - 100% retention charge.

🌿 No-show - 100% retention charge.

*Terms and Conditions*
🌿 Bookings are non-transferable.
